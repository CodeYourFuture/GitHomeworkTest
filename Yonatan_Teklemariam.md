# About Me
Name: **Yonatan Teklemariam**
Location: Originally from Eritrea, now based in Luton, UK

 # Favourite Sport
My favourite sport is cycling. I grew up watching Eritrean cyclists race and win across the world. Their determination inspired me deeply when I was in high school. I even joined my school’s cycling team and participated in my first 30km race. I didn’t win, but the experience was unforgettable and filled me with joy. Cycling has stayed close to my heart ever since.

 # Favourite Food
I’m very connected to my traditional cuisine. My number one favourite dish is porridge served with yoghurt and pepper. It’s simple, comforting, and incredibly energising. The mix of proteins and carbohydrates makes it a powerful meal, and it always leaves me feeling full and refreshed.

#  Interesting Fact About Me
I’m overwhelmingly determined and dedicated to the things I believe in, whether it’s a vision, a promise, or a personal goal. I love sharing ideas, especially subtle or complex ones about life. It may sound philosophical, but I’m not actually a big fan of formal philosophy. I just enjoy deep thinking.

One thing that truly impresses me, even about myself, is how much I admire people who give their lives for the sake of others. That kind of selflessness shapes the way I see the world.
