Favourite Sport and reason for liking: 
My favourite sport is Badminton. I like it because badminton is a fun and very competitive sport. It is easy to learn, but hard to master because it takes practice to develop strong skills and good technique. One of the great things about badminton is that it can be played indoors all year round. It is also excellent for fitness and coordination, as it improves speed, agility, and hand-eye coordination while keeping me active.
