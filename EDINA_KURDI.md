**Favourite sport**

- I love BJJ. Unfortunately, for various reasons I have not been able to go back to practice for a year now, but I hope to in the future. **Favourite food**


I don't have a favourite food. I enjoy food above all else. I think it is one of the best things in life. However, I don't have one single food that rules them all. I am drawn to curries and Asian dishes in general. The flavour profile of the East is just amazing. I love the umami flavours of mushrooms, soy sauce, miso, gochujang, and the mixing of savoury with sweet and spicy (just a little though).


I cook from scratch. I always have, and I aim to cook something different every day, although that is just the aim. I want to try everything that exists, within limits though, as I don't consider animals food.


I really enjoy experimenting with unusual recipes, such as making a cake from millet or chickpeas.
