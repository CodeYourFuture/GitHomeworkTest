   <b> 2. Your favourite sport and why you like it: 

    I don't think of it as a sport, but in terms of movement: I love dancing. Mainly jazz (lindy hop, charleston)/blues. Or just generally throwing myself around a dancefloor. 

    I like dance because you can immediately connect to people - without language barriers &/or needing to use words to 'get to know' them first. 

    Also because it has transformed how I view mistakes - as opportunities to learn, be adaptable, and create something new.
