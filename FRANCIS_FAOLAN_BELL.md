   <b 1. Your name:>

     Francis Faolán Bell (known as Faolán, which is Irish for 'Little Wolf').

   <b 2. Your favourite sport and why you like it: >

    I don't think of it as a sport, but I love dancing. Mainly, vernacular jazz, lindy hop, blues, &/or just throwing myself around a dancefloor to disco music. I like dance because you can connect to people without language and because it has transformed how I view mistakes - as opportunities to learn, be adaptable, and create something new.

   <b 3. Your favourite food and why it's your favourite: >
    
    I love Affogato because it combines three of my favourite things: ice cream, strong black coffee, & getting involved in the process (aka, pouring the coffee on the ice cream).
